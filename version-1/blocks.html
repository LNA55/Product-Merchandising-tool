Manage the Merch blocks

All the merchandising anchor points of the site. Click a block to expand its details: position in the page, ownership, cumulative KPIs, rank and merchandising mix history.

Légende orange : les éléments en orange ont été ajoutés par Claude de sa propre initiative — à valider ou supprimer.

KPI definitions
Displayed
Number of times a page was loaded containing this block.
Viewed
Page loads where the block was above the waterline, plus views tracked by heat-map-like data for anything under the waterline — with specific data for mobile and desktop.
Clicked
Clicks on the products of the block.
Add to cart attributed
Take a user who, in one session, clicks on at least one of the products from this specific merch block: this is the ratio of the products they clicked on that they finally add to cart in the same session.
